FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?+
No. Search a title and read. AnyBook stays free, with no sign up and no card.
Does AnyBook cover fiction and nonfiction?+
Yes. Search any title in either category.
What if my book isn't in the library yet?+
Use the "Request a title" link and send us the title. We read the book ourselves and add the summary — we don't generate it automatically, so it isn't instant, but we work through requests as they come in.
Is the summary biased toward the author's argument?+
No. Each summary includes pushback from critics, not the author's view alone.
Do you offer audio?+
Not yet. Audio narration is on the way, but every summary is text-only for now.
Do you support languages other than English?+
English today. Additional languages come later this year.
